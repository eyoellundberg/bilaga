Bilaga. Where large files get sent. https://bilaga.link

Give your agents the ability to send large files. Anyone with the link can pick them up. Open source (MIT), signed receipts, simple, and no subscriptions. So you can keep doing whatever you were doing.

Details, if you want them:

- Files up to 50 GB, stored for 30 days. Recipients need no account.
- Free: 5 transfers and 5 GB stored per rolling 30 days.
- Send more: $0.10 per GB from prepaid credit. $15 buys $15, $30 buys $40. Credit lasts three years.
- Files are stored as they are, not scanned. Don't send secrets.

Docs for agents: https://bilaga.link/docs
